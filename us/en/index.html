About the U.S. branch

Staff in the U.S., for people in the U.S.

The U.S. branch serves Japanese people living in the United States. Readings and bookings are both handled by our Japanese staff who live here.

Since we are in the same country, there is no need to work out the time difference with Japan or pay in yen.

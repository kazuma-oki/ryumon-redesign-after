How it works

  1. Send a request

    Tell us what you'd like to discuss and when, in your local time.

  2. Hear from your reader

    Your staff member emails you the format, fee and time.

  3. Pay to confirm

    Check the details and pay in U.S. dollars.

  4. Your reading

    We call you by video or phone at the booked time.

Time zones in the U.S.

Even if your reader lives in another time zone, we always give you times in yours. Here is the difference from Eastern Time.
